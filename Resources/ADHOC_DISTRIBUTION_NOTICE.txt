TURNRING FREE / AD-HOC BUILD

This copy has a cryptographic integrity seal, but it is not signed or
notarized by an Apple-trusted Developer ID. macOS cannot confirm who produced
it, and Gatekeeper may reject it.

Before opening Turnring:

1. Obtain the Turnring release public key or its SHA-256 fingerprint through
   an independent trusted channel.
2. Verify the detached signature and checksum using verify-download.sh.
3. Do not disable Gatekeeper globally and do not run copies from unknown
   mirrors.

An Apple Developer ID build is the only supported way to give macOS a
platform-trusted third-party publisher identity outside the Mac App Store.
